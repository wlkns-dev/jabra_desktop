# Jabra Desktop 0.5.2

The device detail panel now shows read-only product information when the
connected device supplies it: part number on headsets and speakers, audio
device name on dongles, and the connected phone on headsets. If a headset
reports an empty phone entry, the panel says that no phone is connected.
Unsupported or unreadable values stay hidden. The new labels update when the
app switches between German and English.

The information follows the selected device. An endpoint shown beneath a
dongle receives only its own product data, including when the SDK finishes a
read after the pairing list has already loaded. Pairing, connection and device
name actions remain available as before. This release does not change device
settings or firmware.

## Verification and limits

On CachyOS/KDE, a Link 370 and Link 380 returned their audio names. An Evolve
75 connected through Link 370 returned its part number and an empty phone
entry; the 0.5.2 app visibly showed the corresponding part number and “No
phone connected” text under that device. An initial launch immediately after
using JabraCLI reported a closed Device Connector; a clean restart recovered
both dongles and the connected endpoints. The cause of that first error is
unconfirmed. Other models and runtime behavior on Ubuntu were not tested.

See the [verification report](../verification-0.5.2.md) and
[hardware matrix](../hardware-validation.md) for details.
